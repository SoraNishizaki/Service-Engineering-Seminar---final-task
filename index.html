About the Research

This project explores non-contact, privacy-preserving ways to watch over elderly residents at home and in community facilities. Millimeter-wave radar tracks movement and breathing patterns without using a camera, while PIR presence and environment sensors add complementary context such as room temperature and humidity.

The system is designed to notice signs that matter for safety and wellbeing — unusual movement, irregular breathing, unexpected night bed-leaving, and gradual changes in sleep rhythm — and has been validated through real-world pilot deployments rather than lab tests alone.
